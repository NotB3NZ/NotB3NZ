## 🧬 About me

I'm Benz, a **BS Data Science student at the University of the Philippines**, with an expected graduation in **2028**. I'm passionate about applying data science to health and understanding the people behind the data.

What I love about data and machine learning is how they help us look beyond a spreadsheet: uncovering patterns, asking better questions, and making sense of what the numbers mean.

Alongside my studies, I freelance as a **Data Analyst and ML Engineer**, working on **data cleaning, processing, and forecasting**. I'm building toward a career in **Data and ML Engineering**, with an interest in the pipelines and systems that turn analysis into something useful.

## 🔬 What I'm focused on

- **Health data science** — exploring questions where data connects with human health.
- **Histopathological data & medical image processing** — working with image data in the context of **Computer-Aided Detection (CAD) systems**.
- **Cloud analytics & data pipelines** — applying data engineering fundamentals and AWS tools to scalable workflows.

## ☁️ Community & leadership

I'm the **Data Analytics Head at AWSCC UP-Min**, where I lead initiatives that help students explore cloud analytics. My focus is on using AWS data analytics tools to build scalable data pipelines and support community projects.

My work brings together **statistical analysis, exploratory data analysis (EDA), machine learning, and data engineering fundamentals**.
